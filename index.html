The challenge

A poem is not a visual specification.

Classical poetry compresses people, events, cultural references and emotions into a few lines. Rendering the visible objects alone can produce beautiful imagery while losing what the poem means.

PoVi reconstructs the implicit context and source–target relations first. That understanding becomes an evidence-bounded plan for narration, visual direction and synchronized audiovisual generation.

  1. 诗

    The input

    A few lines. A whole world.

    A poem leaves its people, histories and figurative relations partly unstated.

  2. 意

    The missing step

    Make the implicit explicit.

    Recover context, connect metaphor to meaning, and preserve interpretive uncertainty.

  3. 影

    The output

    A story to hear. A world to see.

    Direct coherent scenes and synchronize their timing with spoken narration.

Framework

Ground the meaning. Then direct the scene.

01 · Understand

Evidence-bounded planning

A semantic director graph connects context, causal relations, metaphor and visual constraints. Claims cannot exceed the authority of their supporting evidence.

02 · Direct

Role-separated realization

Five specialized stages create the background story, narration, close reading, recurring characters and shot descriptions through validated artifacts.

03 · Synchronize

Audio-clocked assembly

Measured speech duration sets the final clock. Shot allocations, clips, narration and transitions are aligned to the synthesized audio.

Evidence before certainty

A recalled claim is not a documented fact.

Schema, reference and grounding checks validate the graph. Model-recalled knowledge remains plausible or contested; consensus requires high-authority evidence. Failed checks trigger directed repair.

  1. LowModel recollectionPlausible / contested
  2. MediumPoem & metadataNeeded for “documented”
  3. HighScholarly evidenceNeeded for “consensus”

Authority sets an admissibility threshold, not proof of truth or consensus. The evidence inventory is fixed before planning; validation checks citations, structure and line coverage.

Measured speech is the clock

The voice sets the pace of the film.

Provisional shot durations are rescaled after speech synthesis. The final visual sequence follows the measured audio track.

Scope & limitations

A richer process. A measurable cost.

PoVi uses eight initial model calls per poem, compared with one for the baseline. The 120-work benchmark cannot span the full historical and formal diversity of classical Chinese poetry.

Gains are not uniform: DeepSeek’s prelude–explication linkage (D2) falls from 0.825 to 0.569. The automatic evaluator shows moderate human agreement and saturation on some visual metrics. The supplied draft does not specify human-rater count, background or inter-rater agreement.

Eight initial model calls establish the cost of structured planning before any repairs.
